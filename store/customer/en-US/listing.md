# Suqya (Customer) — English store listing

**Title:** Suqya — Water Tanker Delivery  
**Short description (≤80):** Order water tankers in Riyadh. Track delivery live. Pay securely.  
**Full description:**

Suqya (سقيا) helps households and businesses order water tanker deliveries in Saudi Arabia.

- Place an order in a few taps with bilingual Arabic/English UI  
- Live driver tracking on the map  
- Pay cash on delivery or card via Moyasar  
- Tax invoices with QR for your records  
- Manage addresses and order history
